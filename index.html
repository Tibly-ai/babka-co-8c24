Our Story

Started in a two-bedroom kitchen, six years ago.

Babka & Co. began with a single tray of loaves sold out of a cardboard box at the Sunday farmers market. The dough recipe came from my grandmother's notebook, written in Polish, half the measurements in handfuls instead of cups. We've kept it exactly as she wrote it.

These days we bake out of a proper oven on Orchard Street, but the process hasn't changed. Dough proofs overnight, every loaf is twisted by hand, and we bake in small batches so nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting to be sold.

What We Bake

Four swirls, one dough recipe we've never changed.

Classic Chocolate

Dark chocolate ganache folded through buttery dough, finished with a cocoa sugar glaze.

Cinnamon Sugar

Brown butter, Ceylon cinnamon, and a crackled sugar top. Our best seller since day one.

Salted Caramel Pecan

Toasted pecans folded into a dark caramel swirl, finished with flaky sea salt.

Seasonal: Sour Cherry

Tart Montmorency cherries and a hint of almond, on rotation through late summer.
